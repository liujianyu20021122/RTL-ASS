# RTL-ASS 1.3.0

RTL-ASS 1.3.0 focuses on bounded verification decisions and measurable knowledge use while keeping Codex as the RTL engineer.

## Highlights

- Codex can record a strict task-scoped verification plan with concrete required/optional claims, evidence classes, a retry budget, and maximum EDA concurrency of one.
- `verify summarize --require-ready` revalidates explicit current evidence links, reports missing claims, duplicate unchanged evidence, and retry excess, then provides an unambiguous stopping gate.
- High-cost verification and waveform commands share one bounded per-workspace POSIX lock, preventing accidental concurrent EDA launches from the same task.
- Knowledge searches can write immutable retrieval receipts. The receipt binds the actor, query, explicit namespaces, all/any match mode, filters, top-k, ordered results, content hashes, provenance, license state, and rank.
- The isolated evaluator separately observes retrieval results, successful `kb show --include-content` reads, duplicate evidence, and EDA activity after the stopping gate.
- A paired retrieval ablation keeps the Skill, model, prompt, public fixture, hidden grader, open tools, and resource policy fixed while changing only an empty versus populated evaluation namespace. Packs are bounded and rejected when they reuse task artifact hashes, answer-like roles, workflow-case paths, or lack a semantic contamination-review marker.
- Task-bound relevant and plausible-irrelevant treatment manifests separate the complete product effect from the card contribution. Workflow policy 1.4 requires relevant-card inspection while allowing an auditable irrelevant lexical hit to be rejected before full-content loading.
- Verilator binary simulation records warning output but no longer lets warning-only compilation suppress an otherwise executable self-checking simulation; syntax/elaboration errors and failing simulations remain failures.

## Verification boundary

The frozen signed-width campaign completed five product pairs, five Skill-with-empty versus Skill-with-relevant
pairs, and five Skill-with-empty versus Skill-with-plausible-irrelevant pairs. Every candidate was independently
correct and complete. The product treatment improved strict-lint discipline in this task but added substantial
time and tokens. Holding the Skill constant, the relevant card reduced aggregate elapsed time by 2.27%, a modest
task-specific improvement; it was faster in two of five pairs and increased input/output tokens by 3.16%/2.63%.
Correctness remained 5/5 in both conditions.

The exact protocol, costs, hashes, exclusions, and original interpretation are retained in the
[frozen causal retrieval campaign](../../evals/results/2026-09-05-frozen-causal-retrieval-campaign.md). The earlier
[v1.3.0 retrieval ablation](../../evals/results/2026-09-03-v1.3.0-retrieval-ablation.md) remains historical
mechanism evidence under its original raw-pack policy.

Subsequent source-file development results and their pending corrected-grader replay are tracked separately in
the [current evaluation summary](../evaluation.md#measured-effects-and-scope); they do not change the frozen
release-campaign results.

Verification plans reflect claims selected by Codex; they do not prove that the selected claims are sufficient for every unmodeled risk. A successful stopping gate means only that all declared required claims have matching current evidence. Formal and equivalence results remain bounded to their recorded scope, and STA still requires a real mapped netlist, Liberty library, constraints, and OpenSTA execution.
